# UI pass: logbook statistics

Room: #feat-0.2.5-analytics · Pull request [#83](https://github.com/CruxCoach/CruxCoach/pull/83) (follow-up to #48, into the 0.2.5 release branch) · Part of [#44](https://github.com/CruxCoach/CruxCoach/issues/44)

## How

- Every screen and state of the statistics rendered with a Compose UI review test (`StatsUiReviewTest`): the logbook top with entries, small logbooks (first week, only attempts, nothing in the last 30 days), the empty logbook, the logbook filters, the statistics sheet with every chart view, one board picked, a three-year logbook, a first week, only attempts, an empty 30-day period, tapped rows, the session recap (busy, small, attempts only) and the end-of-session summary.
- Each in 16 variants: dark and light, English and German, font scale 1.0 and 2.0, 320 dp and 411 dp wide. All logbooks are made up.
- Four render cycles. After the first one a fresh reviewer that had not seen the code critiqued the renders against the app's design guide; its findings were checked and most of them taken.
- The check of the test build on the test phone follows (gestures, scrolling, sheets).

**Largest font, 320 dp.** Words broke in the middle ("Schwerst|e Begehun|g"), grade labels lost their "+" (6b+ shown as 6b), chart axes and board names were cut, and the logbook entries collapsed into a column of single letters.

- Rows of numbers take as many columns as keep their labels' words whole, so they become two by two or one column at large fonts.

- Board names stand above their bars when they do not fit beside them; the board feel axis drops its ±1 ticks when they would collide.

**At every size.**

- A period without entries said nothing at all; now it says so and offers the whole logbook.

- The "All" board chip no longer repeats the totals of the six numbers right below it.

## Second review (8 November)

A second fresh reviewer looked at the final renders and found one more serious break: on a 320 dp phone at normal font size the entries still collapsed, because their result took the row. Entries now also stack whenever the result would leave the name less than 96 dp, and show result and stars on one line. The end-of-session summary got the same care at large fonts (zone bars on two lines, values below their labels, tiles that wrap), the value labels of "Tries per grade" no longer overlap, and hollow bars of small samples show their counts.

## Review round (9 November)

The maintainer's review of #83 and #86 asked for two things in the logbook and two in the loading of the statistics.

**One layout per list.** Each entry used to decide on its own whether to stack, so in German at 360 dp "Offen · 3 Versuche" stacked while "Flash" stayed in one row. Deciding once per list by stacking every card would have doubled every entry's height even at 411 dp in German, because almost every real logbook has an open project with a few tries. The list now picks one of three layouts from its widest result: the result beside the name when every result fits; the result under the name, still in one row with the checkbox, grade and edit button, when one does not; and the stacked card only at large fonts or when the name would get less than 96 dp. A list only moves to a narrower layout, so loading more entries can't make the cards on screen flip back.

## Review round 2 (9 November)

After #83 was merged, #86 was rebased onto the release branch and got three more changes from the maintainer's review. Checked by adb on test build 5 of #86 (commit ec73b0170).

**The year in review below the six numbers.** Its card loads after the statistics. At the top of the sheet it pushed the chips and the six numbers down when it arrived. It now sits below the six numbers, the period's bests and the empty message, so it stays out of the way while the sheet loads and nothing above the numbers moves.

## Follow-up: one logbook read at a time (9 November)

Pull request #111, from the maintainer's note at #86's merge. Checked by adb on its test build 1 (commit 4c08f542b) in a fresh app with the long test logbook (2,580 sends, 458 attempts).

**Reopening the sheet after a failed load** shows the loading sign again while the logbook is read, and a period or board tap during that read waits for it instead of starting a second one. A failed read can't be forced on the phone, so a unit test covers this.

**Opening the sheet while the first read still runs** starts no second read of the whole logbook. On the phone the list was shown but the overview not yet: the sheet opened with the loading sign, "30 Tage" was tapped while it loaded, and the numbers then came in for 30 days and matched the overview's last 30 days.

## Logbook refresh after a catalogue import (9 November)

Pull request #115 for issue #113. After every catalogue import, the logbook's climbs get the catalogue's names and grades. A climb the catalogue lacks cost a scan of the whole catalogue each, inside a transaction the logbook waits for. The pull request reads the catalogue outside that transaction, and the missing climbs of a batch share one scan.

Same check before and after, on the test phone: a fresh app, the Kilter catalogue, and the long test logbook (2,565 distinct made-up climbs, none in the catalogue) imported while the catalogue loads.

## Not changed

- The overview's link drops its word from font scale 1.5 on (the whole card opens the statistics); kept as decided in the review of #48.
- The shared recap image follows the phone's font size, as on screen.
- Compact chart rows (about 30 dp) stay; a whole row is the tap target.
- Older parts the pass touched only where they broke: the chart view selector, the filter chip styles, the end-of-session zones card.
- Noted by the reviewers, left for a decision: the result colours of the entries (green, orange, red) are below 4.5:1 contrast on the light theme; "tries" and "attempts" both appear in English, "Härtester Send" next to "Schwerste Begehung" in German; the zones card and the grade bars of the end-of-session summary use the same colours for different groupings; the recap tiles say "1 Sends".
